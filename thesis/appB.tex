\label{sec:app-spi-mf}

Here, we show different combinations of values for the stepsize
and $\lambda$ under \emph{large} inflation. Excluded are the tables
for $\lambda = 400$, as no rank reduction could be observed ($\lambda$ too low),
as well as $\lambda = 4 \times 10^{6}$, as here all produced
controllers were unstable ($\lambda$ too high).

From the remainders, we picked a value that exhibits rank reduction, while
at the same time having a low suboptimality.

\subsection{Learning Rates for the RL Solvers}
\label{subsec:app-rl-learning-rate}

For each solver and inflation size, we train three seeds with each initial learning rate
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:app-rl-learning-rate-grid}
  \eta_0 \in 3 \cdot 10^{-4} \sqrt{\frac{10}{d_{\mathrm{in}}}} \cdot \left\{ 0.01,\ \tfrac{1}{30},\ 0.1,\ \tfrac{1}{3},\ 1 \right\} ,
\end{equation}
rounded to two digits. The final rates were then chosen based on inspection of the training curves (\cref{tab:app-rl-learning-rates}).
For Linear PPO under \emph{large} every gain becomes unstable during training (\cref{fig:app-rl-learning-rate-ppo-linear-large}).

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Initial learning rate $\eta_0$ of the PPO solvers per inflation size.}
  \label{tab:app-rl-learning-rates}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
    \toprule
    Solver & \emph{small} & \emph{medium} & \emph{large} \\
    \midrule
    Linear PPO & $1 \cdot 10^{-4}$ & $4.5 \cdot 10^{-6}$ & $2.2 \cdot 10^{-6}$ \\
    Bottleneck PPO & $3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ & $4.5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ & $6.7 \cdot 10^{-6}$ \\
    State encoder + PPO & $1 \cdot 10^{-4}$ & $4.5 \cdot 10^{-5}$ & $6.7 \cdot 10^{-6}$ \\
    Scalable encoder + PPO & $1 \cdot 10^{-4}$ & $1.3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ & $2.2 \cdot 10^{-6}$ \\
    Fully-connected PPO & $3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ & $1.3 \cdot 10^{-5}$ & $6.7 \cdot 10^{-6}$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}
